Decisions
- Menus (ui.js showMenu): one panel that stays; side menus open on hover/click/→ beside the row; `keep:true` rows
  leave the menu open and repaint ticks; label/hint/submenu may be functions. No pop animation, no auto-focused row.
- Highway top bar: parts + Sing + Karaoke + Piano are always there, fixed width; `playAs(mode, partId)` in
  highway.js is the single switch. Go-anywhere button (grid icon) beside Back. `actions.playAs(id, mode)` +
  `state.modeNext` for opening a song in a mode from other pages.
- Top bar no longer fades while the mouse is on a menu (wake listeners on document + 'menu-close' event): that fade
  was one of the flickers.
- Piano prefs: colours (neon default), glow, backdrop (dark default), size (88 default), names.
- Tuning card: practice.js skips it when the song opens as karaoke/piano; highway `asideChanged()` runs it on return.

Status: see the bottom of HANDOFF's 4.18 entry when written. Helpers (subagents) used: Voice fixer, Quest finder,
Feedback researcher, Tone checker.
