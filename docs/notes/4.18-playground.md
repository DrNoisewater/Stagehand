# 4.18 work notes (branch claude/clever-ritchie-xt92un) — keep short, update as it goes

What the user asked (2026-11-14, on 4.17.1), in their words, shortened:
1. Can't find Sing from the highway; switch between guitar / sing / karaoke / piano from anywhere. "UI/UX is core.
   Everything easily doable from everywhere. One ecosystem." Menus and placement critical, "easy to get to anything
   at a glance".
2. Flickering everywhere when touching menus; side menus only open on a click. "Flicker free everywhere". Check the
   blur issue (v3.css 4.12 note: blur over the moving picture flickers on Windows).
3. Piano: up to 88 keys; a modern glowing look like the piano videos (photos: violet/gold glow; rainbow); options.
4. The tuning card shows after a karaoke song starts: show it when switching to the highway (guitar) instead.
5. Voice: hard to hear own voice at all; a small delay ("super fast and accurate"); doesn't understand auto-tune,
   wants the voice carried up to the right note live, Hard tune like T-Pain. Voice changer: for fun, in a relevant
   spot, more presets; Chipmunk "sounded like an old lady"; Giant is cool but should be named like Jigsaw.
6. Singing lane: the bars move up and down too much to tell what note to sing; same pitch should stay put.
7. Quest: the page never loaded (Pi-hole at home; PianoVision works).
8. Look at how "Feedback" does piano ("Virtuoso"?) and vocals; what could we do better.
9. Do tones pulled from songs go through the same sound processing as the gear? Could they sound better?
10. The Drop pedal sometimes shows by itself on the highway.

Decisions
- Menus (ui.js showMenu): one panel that stays; side menus open on hover/click/→ beside the row; `keep:true` rows
  leave the menu open and repaint ticks; label/hint/submenu may be functions. No pop animation, no auto-focused row.
- Highway top bar: parts + Sing + Karaoke + Piano are always there, fixed width; `playAs(mode, partId)` in
  highway.js is the single switch. Go-anywhere button (grid icon) beside Back. `actions.playAs(id, mode)` +
  `state.modeNext` for opening a song in a mode from other pages.
- Top bar no longer fades while the mouse is on a menu (wake listeners on document + 'menu-close' event): that fade
  was one of the flickers.
- Piano prefs: colours (neon default), glow, backdrop (dark default), size (88 default), names.
- Tuning card: practice.js skips it when the song opens as karaoke/piano; highway `asideChanged()` runs it on return.

Status: see the bottom of HANDOFF's 4.18 entry when written. Helpers (subagents) used: Voice fixer, Quest finder,
Feedback researcher, Tone checker.
